- **Participants:** Not announced teams that do not receive a direct VRS playoff place.

- **Match format:** Best-of-One (BO1).

- **Advancement:** The first Not announced teams to meet the qualification condition advance to the Playoffs.

- Swiss matches may be rescheduled only within the published round window, with mutual agreement and QSL administration approval where required by the Rulebook.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1.** *Do all players need a Challengermode account and Not announced?*  
Yes. Every player must use their own eligible Challengermode account and have **Not announced** running for official matches.

**Q2.** *How does VRS qualification work?*  
Not announced eligible, registered teams with the highest rankings in the fixed **Not announced** snapshot receive direct places in the Playoffs. These are direct playoff qualifications within an open-registration tournament, not invitations to participate.

**Q3.** *What happens to teams that do not receive a direct VRS playoff place?*  
The Not announced teams that do not receive a direct VRS playoff place compete in the Swiss Stage. Qualification: A team qualifies after recording 3 match wins. Elimination: A team is eliminated after recording 3 match losses. Advancement: Not announced teams qualify from the Swiss Stage to complete the Not announced-team Playoff field.

**Q4.** *What counts as a team's core?*  
For a VRS-matched team, the reference roster is the five players on its matched VRS roster in the **Not announced** snapshot. For a non-VRS team, it is the five original registered starters. At least three players from the applicable reference roster must play the entirety of every match.

**Q5.** *Can we change our roster?*  
Roster changes require QSL administration approval where specified and do not change the fixed reference roster. During the Playoffs, up to two changes to the Sub 1 / Sub 2 slots are permitted in total, subject to approval.

**Q6.** *Does using an already registered substitute count as a roster change?*  
Simply fielding a substitute already listed on the approved roster does not count as a substitute-slot roster change. Every lineup must still satisfy the applicable three-of-five requirement.

**Q7.** *Can a player be substituted during a match?*  
Substitutions are allowed only between maps, never during a live map.

**Q8.** *What if a player disconnects?*  
If a player disconnects before the first kill of round one, the round is restarted. After that, play continues; the team should take a technical pause and wait for the player to reconnect. If the player cannot return within the pause limit, the team may bring in a registered substitute or continue 4v5.

**Q9.** *Can I stream my POV or cast a match?*  
Player POV streaming may be allowed subject to integrity conditions. Community broadcasting and co-streaming require prior approval from QSL.

**Q10.** *How do I submit a protest?*  
Report disputes during the match by pausing and contacting an admin, with evidence where possible.

**Q11.** *Who makes the final decision on an issue not covered here?*  
QSL tournament administration, applying the principles of competitive integrity and fairness.
