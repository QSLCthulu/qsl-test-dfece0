## 2. Registration and Entry Fee

- The entry fee is **0 USD per team**.
- The tournament is limited to **16 accepted teams**. Registration may close early if all team slots are filled.
- Registration is not complete until the required registration and payment steps have been completed.
- Payment instructions and the registration link will be published through official Quickshot League channels.
- Teams must submit an eligible roster and accurate player/account information.

## 3. Roster and Eligibility

- The tournament is intended for teams competing in the **Global region**.
- All players must use their own registered Steam and Challengermode accounts.
- Not announced is mandatory for all official matches.
- Account sharing, unauthorized stand-ins, cheating, and attempts to bypass tournament integrity checks are prohibited.
- Roster changes and substitutions must comply with the Rulebook and receive administrative approval where required.

### 3.1. Roster & Core Policy

Rosters lock at check-in. Changes after that need admin approval.

### 3.2. VRS-Matched Teams

- A VRS-matched team must field at least **three (3) of the five players** listed on its matched VRS roster in the Not announced snapshot for the entirety of every match, including playoffs.
- Any three of those five players may satisfy this; the same three are not required in every match.
- Teams must keep at least three of those snapshot players on their official roster throughout the tournament. Falling below three may result in disqualification. A match with fewer than three snapshot players fielded is a forfeit.
- These rules apply regardless of approved roster changes or substitutions.

### 3.3. Non-VRS Teams

- A non-VRS team must field at least **three (3) of its five original registered starters** for the entirety of every match, including playoffs.
- Any three of those original starters may satisfy this; the same three are not required in every match.

### 3.4. Duplicate Core Restrictions

- A player may only appear on one team's roster in this tournament.
- Teams violating this may be disqualified.

### 3.5. Roster Changes & Substitutions

- **Swiss Stage:** Rosters lock at check-in. Changes after that need admin approval.
- **Playoffs:** Roster changes after check-in require admin approval per the Rulebook, and every lineup must satisfy the three-of-five requirement.
- Using an already registered substitute requires admin approval.

### 3.6. Coach Substitution Rule

Registered team coaches may substitute as players only if listed on the official roster before the match starts, meet eligibility, and the lineup satisfies the applicable three-of-five requirement.

## 4. VRS Direct Qualification

**Not announced teams** qualify directly for the **Not announced-team Playoffs** based on the official VRS ranking snapshot dated **Not announced**.

- Direct places go to the Not announced highest-ranked eligible VRS teams among accepted tournament entrants.
- The ranking snapshot used is the official VRS ranking on **Not announced**.
- Teams must be registered, accepted, and eligible to receive a direct place.
- If a direct-qualifying team is not registered or is ineligible, the place passes to the next-highest-ranked eligible registered team.
- Organizers publish the VRS snapshot date and direct qualifier list before the Swiss Stage begins.

## 5. Swiss Stage

The **Not announced teams** that do not receive a direct playoff place compete in the Swiss Stage.

- Format: **Not announced-team Swiss Stage**
- Match format: Group and early bracket matches are BO1; playoffs and finals are BO3 unless the listing says otherwise.
- Qualification: A team qualifies after recording **Not announced match wins**.
- Elimination: A team is eliminated after recording **Not announced match losses**.
- Advancement: **Not announced teams** qualify from the Swiss Stage to complete the Not announced-team playoff field.
- Teams are paired against opponents with the same or a similar match record.
- Teams should not face the same opponent more than once, except for unavoidable bracket or administrative issues.
- Pairings, seeding, and tiebreak procedures are published before the first round.

## 6. Playoffs

The Playoffs feature **Not announced teams** in a Single-Elimination bracket:

- **Not announced VRS direct qualifiers**
- **Not announced Swiss Stage qualifiers**
- Standard playoff matches: **BO3**
- Grand Final: **BO3**, unless the listing says otherwise
- A team is eliminated after one playoff match loss.
- The bracket and seeding plan will be published by tournament administration after Swiss qualification is complete.

## 7. Seeding Integrity Policy

- VRS matching uses the fixed Not announced snapshot.
- A team is VRS-matched when at least three of its registered starting players appear on the same official VRS roster. Substitutes are not counted for initial matching.
- VRS-matched teams must field at least three players from that matched five-player VRS roster throughout every match.
- Non-VRS teams must field at least three of their five original registered starters throughout every match.
- The three qualifying players may differ between matches.
- Direct playoff qualification is determined by VRS rank among eligible, accepted registrants; it is not an invitation.
- Rosters lock at check-in. Changes after that need admin approval.
- Teams disqualified for roster-continuity violations lose any seeded or direct-qualification position, and administration may award the place to the next eligible team.

## 8. Platform Requirements

All official matches must be played through the designated **Challengermode** tournament infrastructure or another match service explicitly announced by Quickshot League.

## 9. Anti-Cheat & Eligibility

**Not announced is mandatory for every player in official matches.**

- Players must have Anti-Cheat running for the full match as required by the tournament provider.
- A match may not begin if a player fails the Anti-Cheat requirement.
- Cheating, unauthorized third-party software, account sharing, and attempts to bypass Anti-Cheat are prohibited.

## 10. Match Settings

Matches use the provider's official competitive server configuration.

- **Game:** Counter-Strike 2.
- **Regulation format:** MR12.
- Start money, freeze time, and round time follow the tournament provider's official competitive server configuration.
- **Overtime:** At 12–12, play MR3 (best of six rounds) with $10,000 starting money, repeated until a winner is decided.
- **Overtime continuation:** repeated until a winner is decided.
- Only unmodified game files are allowed. Custom HUDs, scripts that automate actions, and exploits are banned.

## 11. Map Veto

- BO3 veto: Team A bans, Team B bans, Team A picks, Team B picks, Team A bans, Team B bans; the last map decides.
- The higher seed is Team A. With no seeding, a coin flip decides.

## 12. Servers and Region

- Tournament region: **Global**
- Tournament location: **Online**
- Matches use the server selected by Challengermode or designated by administration for that match.
- Players are responsible for ensuring their internet connection, hardware, and software are suitable for online competition.

## 13. Match Integrity & Administration

All participants must compete fairly and professionally. Prohibited conduct includes cheating, using unauthorized third-party software, sharing accounts or using unauthorized stand-ins, stream sniping or receiving outside information during a match, exploiting game bugs, match-fixing, intentionally manipulating results, harassment, hate speech, threats, discrimination, or intentional match disruption. Players, coaches, and staff may not bet on any match in a tournament they take part in, or share private information with anyone who does.

- Teams should preserve demos, screenshots, recordings, and Challengermode match information if an incident or protest occurs.
- Tournament administration has the authority to interpret and enforce the rules, investigate incidents, and issue penalties where appropriate.
